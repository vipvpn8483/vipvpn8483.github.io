Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Strict Zero-Logs Policy

VIP VPN is built on the principle of absolute privacy. We do not track, collect, or store any information regarding your online activities. This means:

  • No Logging of Browsing History: We do not know what websites you visit.
  • No Traffic Destination Logging: We do not log where your internet traffic goes.
  • No DNS Query Logging: We do not track your DNS requests.
  • No Connection Timestamps: We do not log when you connect or disconnect.

2. Data We Do Collect

To operate our network securely and maintain server health, we collect the bare minimum operational data:

  • Aggregated Bandwidth Usage: We track total bandwidth transferred per server to manage network load and prevent infrastructure abuse. This is strictly anonymized and not tied to your individual identity.
  • Device IDs for Ads (Free Tier): If you use the free version of our app, third-party advertising partners (like Google AdMob) may collect standard advertising identifiers to serve personalized ads.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with certain third-party services that have their own Privacy Policies. These include:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution and analytics.
  • Google AdMob: Used to provide advertisements to keep our free tier operational.

4. Law Enforcement and Valid Legal Requests

VIP VPN operates in full compliance with the law. While our strict zero-logs policy prevents us from retaining data regarding your online activities, we do respond to valid, legally-binding subpoenas, court orders, and search warrants.

Because we do not log browsing history, destination IPs, or connection timestamps, we are physically unable to provide authorities with data that does not exist. Any operational data we *do* possess is only surrendered under direct, valid legal obligation from a court of competent jurisdiction.

5. Security of Infrastructure

We utilize enterprise-grade security on our servers to ensure that data in transit is encrypted using state-of-the-art protocols (such as WireGuard®). Our servers are actively monitored for unauthorized access, ensuring the integrity of our zero-logs environment.
